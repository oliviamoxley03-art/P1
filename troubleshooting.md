# Troubleshooting and Modifications 


| **Situation** | **Accomodation** |
| --- | --- | 
| The class period is shorter than 60 minutes. | Break each day into 2 sections. Rather than a 3 day lesson, the section becomes a longer unit that lasts over a week. There is no rush to finish within the allotted time– each classroom runs at its own pace. |
| --- | --- |
| A student did not complete the lesson and needed extended time. | Allow them to continue into the next day's warm-up rather than moving on unfinished — the Expected Result for each day exists so you can tell when this is happening before it compounds. | 
| --- | --- |
| We only have 1-2 planned days to teach this lesson. | You are able to condense Day 1 and 2 into one day’s lesson. You may opt to not write letters and focus on identifying business letter conventions if you experience time constraints. |
